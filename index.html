How We Work

We’re here to make a grounded difference.

The clients we work with carry so much at once; it can be hard to prioritize themselves.

In our Santa Monica office and virtual appointments, your safety and autonomy are our highest priorities. We listen with clinical precision and warmth to tailor every session to your history, rather than following rigid one-size-fits-all scripts.

We gently weave together cognitive clarity (CBT), deep physiological healing (EMDR and somatic grounding), and practical relational boundaries. Our goal is to walk alongside you with steady compassion, equipping you to reconnect with your inherent calm and strength.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer in-person and telehealth sessions? +

Yes. We welcome clients in-person at our serene private suite in Santa Monica, California. We also provide secure, HIPAA-compliant video telehealth sessions for clients living anywhere across California.

What is your clinical approach to trauma and EMDR? +

We prioritize physical and emotional safety first. Before beginning EMDR reprocessing, we ensure you have reliable somatic grounding and distress tolerance resources. The pace is always collaborative and dictated by your nervous system’s comfort.

How do I get started with therapy? +

You can schedule a complimentary 15-minute phone consultation using our online contact form. This conversation allows us to discuss your goals, answer questions about insurance/rates, and confirm that we are the right therapeutic match.

Do you accept insurance? +

Dr. Maya Reynolds is an out-of-network provider. We provide monthly comprehensive "superbills" which you may submit directly to your PPO insurance for partial out-of-network reimbursement. A Good Faith Estimate is provided to all clients.
